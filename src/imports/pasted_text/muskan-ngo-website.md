Design a complete NGO website for Muskan, an Indian women's empowerment NGO focused on menstrual equity. The website should feel emotionally moving, warm, and hope-driven — not corporate or clinical.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Use a soft pastel colour palette: blush pink (#F9E8EC), blush mid (#F0C4CF), blush deep (#D4768F), rose (#C45C78), rose dark (#8B3A52), warm cream (#FDF8F3), cream mid (#F5EDE4), sage green (#B5C9B0), text dark (#2D1F1F). Page background: #FDF8F3.

TYPOGRAPHY
Display font: Cormorant Garamond — used for all headlines, section titles, pull quotes, and hero text. Weights: 400, 600 italic. Body font: DM Sans — used for all body copy, navigation, labels, captions. Weights: 300 (light) and 400. Hero H1: 5rem, weight 600, line-height 1.1. Section H2: 3rem, weight 600. Card H3: 1.3rem, weight 600. Body: 1rem, weight 300, line-height 1.7. Labels/eyebrows: 0.75rem, weight 500, ALL CAPS, 0.12em letter spacing.

NAVIGATION
Desktop: sticky top bar with frosted glass effect (backdrop-filter blur + 92% cream opacity). Logo image on the left. Centre: nav links — Home, About, Periods, Gallery, Volunteer — in DM Sans, 0.85rem, all-caps, Text Mid colour. Right: "Donate Now" pill button in Rose (#C45C78) with white text. Mobile: hamburger icon triggers a full-height side drawer from the right. Drawer has all links stacked vertically in large text, Donate button full-width at the bottom.

PAGE 1 — HOME
Hero: Full viewport height. Diagonal gradient background (#FDF3F6 → #FDF8F3 → #F5F0EA). Two decorative soft-blurred circles — large blush one top-right, small sage one bottom-left. Content left-aligned: (1) small pill eyebrow tag in blush pink reading "Menstrual Equity · Women's Empowerment" with a rose dot; (2) large Cormorant Garamond Italic headline "1 in 5 girls drops out of school because of her period." with "1 in 5 girls" in rose; (3) DM Sans Light subheading; (4) two pill CTAs — "Join the Movement" (rose filled) and "Our Story →" (rose outline); (5) animated vertical scroll line indicator at bottom centre.
What We Do: Three equal program cards on white background. Each card: 52px icon block (14px radius) in tinted background → H3 title in Cormorant Garamond → body text in DM Sans. Card 1: Pad Distribution (blush icon bg). Card 2: Health Education (sage icon bg). Card 3: Stigma Breaking (cream icon bg). Hover: card lifts 4px, bottom accent bar animates in from left in rose gradient.
Impact Numbers: Full-width section on rose dark (#8B3A52) background. Ghost watermark text "मुस्कान" at 4% opacity top-right. Four equal metric cards with semi-transparent white borders. Numbers in 3.2rem Cormorant Garamond white. Metrics: 3,200+ Girls Reached · 18,000+ Pads Distributed · 42+ Villages & Schools · 6+ Years of Service.
Testimonials: Cream mid background. Three white cards. Each card has a large decorative opening quote character (5rem Cormorant Garamond, blush mid) top-left, followed by a quote in italic Cormorant Garamond, and an author row with a 40px blush avatar circle (rose initial letter), name, and role. Voices: a beneficiary girl in Hindi, a college volunteer, a donor.
Partners Strip: Centred muted label "Supported by". Row of pill-shaped text tags in cream-mid background listing partner names.
CTA Strip: Soft gradient (blush → light pink → cream). Centred Cormorant Garamond headline "Be the reason she stays in school" with "she stays in school" in italic rose. Two pill CTAs: "Volunteer With Us" and "Donate Now".

PAGE 2 — ABOUT
Founder's Story: Two-column layout — warm slightly blush-tinted founder photo (left, 55%) + text block (right): section eyebrow, Cormorant Garamond headline "Why Muskan Exists", 2-3 paragraphs, pull quote with 4px rose left-border accent.
Our Values: 2x2 grid of value cards. Each: large numeral (01–04) in blush, value name in Cormorant Garamond, one-line description. Values: Dignity · Equity · Community · Courage.
Milestones Timeline: Horizontal timeline on desktop, vertical on mobile. Central blush-mid line. Alternating above/below markers: year label (rose, bold) → 12px rose dot → event description (DM Sans 14px muted). Reveals left-to-right on scroll.
Team Profiles: 3 or 4-column grid of cards. Each: portrait photo (rounded top) → name in Cormorant Garamond → role in DM Sans muted → one-liner quote in italic.

PAGE 3 — PERIODS
Why It Matters: Blush background hero. Centred large Cormorant Garamond Italic headline confronting period stigma. 2-3 DM Sans paragraphs below. Rose-bordered pull quote from an activist or expert. Tone: soft activism — warm but urgent.
Myth vs Fact: Paired cards side-by-side. Myth cards: rose-tinted background. Fact cards: sage-tinted background. Three pairs: (1) "Periods are impure" vs biological fact. (2) "Girls should rest" vs light exercise helps cramps. (3) "Pads are a luxury" vs menstrual hygiene is a health right.
Storytelling Block: Full-width editorial photo with soft overlay. First-person story below or overlaid. Pull quote in 1.4rem Cormorant Garamond Italic.
Resources: 3-column download cards. Each: document icon + title + short description + "Download" outline rose button. Resources: menstrual hygiene guide (PDF), myth-busting school poster (PNG), volunteer session toolkit (PDF).

PAGE 4 — GALLERY
Filter pills at top: All, Field Visits, Health Sessions, Pad Distribution, Anganwadi, Hostel Pad Banks. Unselected: blush outline pill. Selected: rose fill, white text. Below: 3-column masonry photo grid, 12px radius on images, warm documentary photography style. Hover: blush overlay with event name revealed at bottom of photo.

PAGE 5 — VOLUNTEER
Why Volunteer: Two-column — left: large editorial volunteer quote with photo and name. Right: 2-3 volunteer impact stats (127 active volunteers, 9 states reached, etc.).
Roles: 3-column grid of role cards. Each: icon (52px) → role title (Cormorant Garamond) → 3 bullet points on involvement → time commitment badge. Roles: On-Ground Volunteer · Social Media Volunteer · Fundraising Volunteer · Design & Content.
Sign-Up Form: Centred white card (max-width 600px) on blush background. Fields: Full Name, Email, Phone, City, Area of Interest (checkboxes: On-Ground / Social Media / Fundraising / Design), Availability (dropdown: Weekends / Weekdays / Both). Submit CTA: "I'm In" — rose pill button, full width.

PAGE 6 — DONATE
Emotional header: Full-width blush section. Cormorant Garamond Italic 3rem headline "Your ₹500 puts 10 pads in a girl's hands." Subline about impact transparency.
Donation widget: Centred white card (max-width 560px) with 4px rose top border. Preset amount pills: ₹200 (pads for 4 girls), ₹500 (pads for 10 girls), ₹1,000 (funds one school session), ₹2,500 (supports a village drive). Selected preset: rose fill + white text. Unselected: blush outline. Open amount input below: "Or enter your own amount".
Payment details: Two-column — left: UPI QR code + UPI ID. Right: bank transfer details.
Trust signals: Three sage-background badge pills — "80G Tax Exemption" · "100% Transparent" · "Registered NGO".
Fund breakdown: Simple horizontal bar showing 70% Programme Delivery, 15% Operations, 15% Outreach.

GLOBAL ELEMENTS
Footer: Dark background (#2D1F1F). Four-column grid — Col 1: logo + tagline + brand description. Col 2: Navigate links. Col 3: Get Involved links. Col 4: Contact details + socials. Bottom bar with copyright left, "Made with love in New Delhi" right.
Floating WhatsApp button: Fixed bottom-right. 52px circle. #25D366 green. White WhatsApp SVG icon. Box shadow in green. Hover: scale up slightly.
Animations: Hero elements stagger fadeUp on load (opacity 0→1, translateY 24px→0, 0.6s ease, staggered 0.15s apart). Cards fade up on scroll via IntersectionObserver. Impact numbers count up on scroll. Program card hover: translateY(-4px) + accent bar scaleX(0→1). Timeline reveals left-to-right.
Responsive: Desktop ≥1024px full layout. Tablet 768–1023px: 2-column grids, reduced font sizes. Mobile <768px: single column, side drawer nav, full-width stacked CTAs.
